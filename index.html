The club

MORE THAN
A CLUB.

Student-run since day one. Open to every level.

GACHON WIND is the university's student-run baseball club. No experience needed — we'll lend you a glove.

We practise twice a week at the Global Campus field, play friendlies against universities across the Seoul area, and welcome players of every level, including complete beginners. Around half our members are international students.

What we do

Twice a week, all season.

01

Weekly practice

Drills, batting practice and scrimmages every Tuesday and Thursday evening. Bats, balls and gloves are provided.

02

Game days

Friendlies and league games against universities around Seoul, home and away. Supporters are always welcome.

03

Campus life

CCA fairs, welcome events and end-of-season gatherings. Find us at the booths each March and September.
